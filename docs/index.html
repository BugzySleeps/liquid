    what's in it

    it replaces waybar, mako, wofi and hyprlock.

    lock screen

    Liquid rises over your blurred wallpaper (a GPU shader), the login box floats on its waves, every key drops a ripple, a wrong password splashes, and unlocking drains it away, each with its own water sound.

    idle tide

    Step away and the liquid slowly rises over your windows, bending them under its surface. Touch anything and it drains with a ripple; let it fill the screen and it becomes the lock screen.

    dynamic island

    The middle of the bar swells down like a stretching drop for the volume (liquid filling across it), a new track, or a notification.

    launcher

    Apps, plus a mode per first character: = maths, : emoji, / files, ! web search, # colours off the screen, > commands.

    music

    The cover blurred through the panel, a waterline visualizer, synced lyrics, cmus's library and queue, and a 10-band eq on everything playing.

    wallpapers recolour everything

    Pick a wallpaper and the bar, panels, borders, terminal and lock screen all take its colours, crossfading live.

    workspace overview

    Every workspace floats on the liquid as a pool of live previews; drag a window between them and it lands with a splash.

    shapes and glass

    Square, round or squiggle for everything at once, with liquid glass (see-through, frosted, a sheen of light) on top.

    sounds

    All synthesised from scratch in Python: the lock screen's water, terminal open and close sounds in five styles, notification sounds.

    and more

    Control centre, calendar, desktop widgets, clipboard history, a window switcher, a screenshot editor, a first-run welcome, and a performance mode.

    use it

    it's the shell half of the ekitai rice: colours, fonts and helper scripts come from the dotfiles.

    git clone https://github.com/BugzySleeps/liquid ~/.config/quickshell/ekitai
qs -c ekitai
